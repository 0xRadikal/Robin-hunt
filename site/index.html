That contract is a test harness, not the collection. It has 2 of 1000 minted, so it can never be verified — on purpose. The real Coinkins collection has not launched, and we will not announce a date until we have the community to support it.

Things we learned the hard way

  • Arc's gas token is USDC at 18 decimals — not 6. Assume 6 and you are wrong by 1012.
  • Arc's SeaDrop is authentic. We byte-diffed it against Ethereum's: 34 bytes differ out of 21,081 — the chainId and the derived EIP-712 domain separator. Nothing else.
  • Do not deploy your own SeaDrop. Our own compile differed in 38.7% of bytes. Use 0x00005EA00Ac477B1030CE78506496e8C2dE24bf5.
  • Never hardcode gas. We watched base fee move 31 → 299 gwei in hours. eth_maxPriorityFeePerGas returns 0 on Arc — trust it and your transaction hangs forever.
  • On-chain metadata is expensive. ~890 characters costs ~697,000 gas. We wasted $0.0126 on an out-of-gas revert learning that.
  • ERC721A token IDs start at 1, not 0.

The uncomfortable part

We scanned 100 NFT collections on Arc. Zero are verified. Zero have any volume. We are not going to pretend there is a crowd here yet.

So we are doing it in the honest order: build the thing, prove it works, publish what we learned, and grow a real community before minting anything. If nobody shows up, we would rather find that out now than sell a thousand items to nobody.
